A community of mums, for mums

No mum should feel alone.

Bloom Together Mums brings mums across Wembley and Brent together for friendship, support and a little time for themselves. Bring your children and come as you are.

What we do

Make friends, play and grow together

Each meet-up is different. Here are a few things we enjoy together.

Coffee & conversation

Share a cuppa, meet another mum and find a listening ear.

Walks & picnics

Get some fresh air, bring the buggy and enjoy time outdoors together.

Play & crafts

From soft play to creative activities, there’s time for children to play while mums connect.

We also make space for wellbeing conversations, seasonal celebrations and occasional guest speakers. If you’re looking for more support, we can help you find local family services.

Meet the founder

Started by a Wembley mum

Vencilla Costa founded Bloom Together Mums after seeing how much it matters to have people around you who listen and understand. As a local mum with experience working with children and families, she wanted to make it easier for mums to find friendship and support close to home.

Whether you’ve just had a baby, moved to the area or simply want some company, there’s a place for you here.

Together, we bloom stronger.

Before you come

Who can join?

Mums from all backgrounds are welcome, including new mums, mums with older children and grandmothers.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. If you’re new and would like someone to welcome you when you arrive, send us a message before the meet-up.

How can I suggest an activity or get involved?

Message us with ideas for future meet-ups or email us to ask about volunteering, hosting a talk or working together.
